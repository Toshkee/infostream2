# Infostream

**Software built to last.** Infostream is a software company from Podgorica,
Montenegro. Since 2004 we have designed, built and operated the information
systems that public institutions and companies rely on every day: from
enterprise information systems to cloud solutions, delivered securely, at
scale, and maintained for the long run.

This repository holds the source of our website, [infostream.co.me](https://infostream.co.me),
available in English (`/eng`) and Montenegrin (`/mne`).

## What we do

- **Software development.** Custom enterprise software designed around the
  client's own processes, delivered incrementally with Agile, and supported
  throughout its lifecycle.
- **Digital transformation.** Assess, digitize, automate, optimize: replacing
  paper-based and fragmented procedures with connected digital workflows.
- **Technology consulting.** Technology assessment, strategic roadmaps and
  solution architecture for confident digital decisions.
- **System integration.** Connecting applications, databases and legacy
  systems into one reliable ecosystem through secure APIs and data
  synchronisation.

## Where we have depth

Twenty years of learning four domains from the inside:

| Domain              | Systems we build                                                             |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Finance             | Taxation, treasury, budgeting, business registries and reporting             |
| Human Resources     | Recruitment, payroll, employee administration and workforce management       |
| DMS & Workflow      | Digital approvals, document traceability and protected data integrity        |
| Healthcare          | Administration, patient data management and control over sensitive records   |

Selected work includes the Central Register of Business Entities for the Tax
Administration of Montenegro, the Treasury information system for the Ministry
of Finance, the Official Gazette legal register, the Innovation Fund platform
and the national register of NGOs and political parties. The full reference
list is on the [Expertise](https://infostream.co.me/eng#expertise) pages.

## How we work

- **Platforms:** Oracle and Microsoft; development primarily in Oracle APEX
  and .NET.
- **Delivery:** Agile, with frequent releases and transparent communication.
- **Security:** ISO 27001 certified, externally audited.
- **Track record:** 60+ systems delivered, 20+ years operating critical state
  systems.
